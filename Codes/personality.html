My Top 3 Traits

  1. Flexible - I am able to to multiple things both inside and out of school to a decent to high level. Because of this I am very comfortable adjusting to whatever role is required of me, whether that be a fielding position in cricket, doing multiple aspects of a group assignment, being able to fill any role in a gaming team, etc. It has helped me massively in school because when something needs to be done I don't worry about how it will be done, as long as I focus I will do it. This, in turn, has contributed to great and exceptional grades in every assignment.
  2. Sociable - I am a very easy-to-get-along-with person, and can fit into almost any social situation. This has allowed me to make lots of friends and acquaintances, making classwork and assignments much easier, especially when working with new people.
  3. Focused - When needs be I can become very focused and complete tasks quickly and efficiently. Throughout school this has allowed me to complete high-quality work within the alotted timeframe, even if I procrastinated before.

Quote from My Hero

My Hero is my father. He, alongside my mother, made a lot of sacrifices to ensure he could be as involved in my life as possible. He has always been involved in everything I love, especially sports (mostly cricket and football) and is always someone I can depend on at any moment. A quote he always tells me is that You must learn to be abase before you learn to be abound. Meaning to be able to understand how to manage much, you must first know how to manage little. This applies in any area of life; financially, socially, etc. Understanding the value of each small thing helps to keep you grounded when more resources flow your way. This is a quote that has and forever will stick with me.

LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch.

As a student, I believe this statement to be completely accurate. Undoubtedly, LLMs give a new level of convenience and assistance that has never been achieved before. Information that students of the past would spend hours searching for is merely one or two prompts away. However, the issue arises when tools made to make learning and education easier to grasp begin to be used as a replacement tool instead. When such a situation arises, it is only at the cost of our own development. Research has raised concerns that relying too heavily on AI-generated responses may affect the development of students' cognitive skills and learning (Korkmaz & Toraman, 2024). Similarly, research on AI text generators in higher education has examined their potential influence on students' critical thinking skills, showing why students should remain actively involved in the thinking process rather than simply accepting AI-generated answers (Kremantzis et al., 2024). To me, I believe LLMs can be used in the creation of projects to help find valid sources and give us pointers that help put us on the right trajectory for our own research and critical thinking, not outright creating projects or doing assignments for us.
